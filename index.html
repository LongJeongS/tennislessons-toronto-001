Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do tennis lessons cost in Toronto?+
Private lessons in Toronto typically range from $70 to $110 per hour depending on coach certification level and venue. Top-tier Performance Stream coaches and prime-time slots sit at the upper end of that range. Semi-private (two students) typically runs $40 to $80 per person per hour. We confirm the exact rate with your matched coach before booking, so there are no surprises.
What's the difference between a Tennis Canada Club Pro and a Coach 2?+
Tennis Canada certification splits into two streams after Club Pro 1: Club Professional 2/3 (club coaching — recreational and developmental players in a club setting) and Coach 2/3/4 (performance stream — competitive players targeting tournaments). Club Pro 3 is the highest club certification; Coach 4 is the highest performance certification short of the national team level. Both are rigorous; the right one depends on what you want from coaching.
How many tennis lessons do I need to play recreationally?+
Most adult beginners reach comfortable rallying after eight to twelve lessons. To play casual matches with friends, twelve to twenty lessons over a season is a realistic benchmark. Competitive juniors aiming for OTA-pathway tournament play typically train two to three times per week year-round. Your coach gives a specific estimate after your first session based on where you're starting.
Should I start with private or group tennis lessons?+
Private lessons reach specific technique goals faster because every minute is on you. Group classes are more affordable and build a community of players at your level. Many of our students do both — group classes for regular play and league building, occasional private lessons for fixing a specific stroke or working with a higher-certified coach. We help you decide during the coach match call.
Are tennis lessons available indoor through Toronto winters?+
Yes. Toronto has stronger indoor tennis capacity than most Canadian cities. Sessions continue through winter at Toronto Lawn Tennis Club, the Cricket Club, Mayfair Clubs across the GTA, Eglinton Flats, BreakPoint at UCC, and other premier indoor facilities. Player development does not pause from November to April.
Where do tennis lessons take place across Toronto?+
Sessions happen at our partner venues across Toronto, North York, East York, Etobicoke, Scarborough, and Leaside. Indoor capacity is concentrated in midtown and North York; outdoor capacity expands to public Park Board courts in spring and summer. Your matched coach proposes the closest premier venue to your neighbourhood.
Is it too late to learn tennis as an adult?+
No — tennis is one of the most age-friendly sports to start as an adult. Our adult-beginner program regulars range from their late twenties to their early sixties. The Tennis Canada certification framework was designed around progressive learning at any age, and the structured drill design means you can pick up the fundamentals at any starting point.
How do I know if a Toronto tennis coach is genuinely top-rated?+
Two things to check. First, certification level — Tennis Canada Club Pro 3 or Coach 2/3/4 are the credentials that matter; anything below Club Pro 1 is entry-level. Second, where they coach — top coaches teach at established clubs like Toronto Lawn TC, the Cricket Club, Mayfair, or BreakPoint. We verify both for every coach in our network.
